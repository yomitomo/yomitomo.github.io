Yomitomo (読み友) Privacy Policy

Last updated: 15 November 2026 · 한국어 · 繁體中文

Yomitomo helps you read Japanese. On a web page you hover (Chrome, Mac Safari) or tap (iPhone Safari) a Japanese word to see its reading and meaning, and you can save words to review later. What you read and the words you save are not sent over the internet to us or to anyone else. The only exceptions are features you turn on yourself, listed below.

Information we collect

None. Yomitomo does not collect personal information, has no accounts and no sign-up, and contains no ads, tracking or analytics.

What is stored only on your device

  • Saved words: the word, its reading and meaning, the sentence it appeared in (up to 200 characters), the page address and title, and when you saved it
  • Settings: on/off, pop-up font size, sites you turned it off on, words per day, and your display language (English / Korean / Traditional Chinese)
  • Study record: for each word the next review date and how often you got it right or wrong; your daily goal and which cards you studied each day (to count your streak)
  • Words you add yourself: words added with ＋ in the app, meanings you edited, and your own notes
  • Files you open (PDF, Word, text): for your 5 most recent files, the text taken from the file and where you stopped reading; for those PDFs, a copy of the file itself so it can reopen in its original layout. Files never leave your device
  • Anki settings (Chrome): whether Anki is connected, whether to send words right away, the deck name, and which words were sent

In Chrome this is kept in the storage Chrome gives the extension (chrome.storage.local) and is removed when you remove the extension. On iPhone and Mac it is kept inside the app and is removed when you delete the app. Exported Anki files are saved only to your own Downloads folder.

How page text is handled

When you hover or tap, Yomitomo reads the Japanese text around that spot (8 characters before, 16 after) and looks it up in the dictionary that is built into Yomitomo. This happens entirely on your device. Page text is never sent anywhere or logged.

Internet connection

The dictionary is built in, so looking up words works offline.

iCloud sync (iPhone and Mac apps)

The iPhone and Mac apps keep your saved words, study record and daily goal in sync between your devices using your own private iCloud storage (Apple CloudKit private database). We cannot see this data and Yomitomo has no server of its own. If you do not use iCloud, everything stays on the device. The Chrome version does not use iCloud.

Sentence translation (iPhone and Mac apps)

On the back of a review card the app can translate the example sentence with Apple's on-device Translation feature. The translation is done by your device; Yomitomo does not send the sentence to us.

iPhone app and Safari extension

On iPhone the Safari extension lives inside the Yomitomo app. Words you save in Safari move to the app only within the same iPhone (a storage area the app and extension share, called an App Group). Allowing the extension on "All Websites" lets it show meanings when you tap Japanese on any site; page text is used only for the on-device dictionary lookup.

Send to Anki (Chrome, only if you turn it on)

If you press "Connect" in settings, Yomitomo sends the word, reading, meaning, sentence, page title and address and JLPT level of the words you save to AnkiConnect, an add-on of the Anki app running on the same computer (address 127.0.0.1:8765, which means this computer itself). This never leaves your computer. Chrome and Anki each ask for your permission the first time. If you never connect, this feature does nothing. If you use Anki's own sync (AnkiWeb), Anki's policy applies from there.

Sending feedback (only if you choose to)

"Send feedback" opens a Google Form (or a new email). Only what you type and submit, plus Yomitomo's version number filled in to help with bugs, reaches the developer. Your saved words and the pages you read are not included. Google's privacy policy applies to what you enter in the form.

Permissions (Chrome)

  • storage: to keep the saved words and settings above
  • activeTab: to know the current site's name when you choose "Turn off on this site"
  • Run on all sites: to show meanings when you hover Japanese on any site
  • 127.0.0.1:8765 (optional): requested only when you connect "Send to Anki", to add cards to Anki on the same computer

Children

Yomitomo collects no personal information from anyone, including children.

Contact

For questions about this policy or Yomitomo, use the "Support" tab on the Chrome Web Store page or email yomitomo.help@gmail.com.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with its date.
